------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------
These guidelines help front office and home collection staff give patients
correct, consistent information before and after their tests.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. WORKING HOURS
------------------------------------------------------------
Main laboratory sample collection: Monday to Saturday, 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
Sunday collection: 7:00 AM to 1:00 PM.
Home collection slots: 6:30 AM to 11:00 AM daily, booked at least 12 hours in advance.
Emergency (STAT) samples are accepted 24 hours a day at the main laboratory.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. PATIENT PREPARATION
------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Fasting tests
- Fasting Blood Sugar: fast for 8 to 10 hours. Plain water is allowed.
- Lipid Profile: fasting for 10 to 12 hours is preferred. Plain water is allowed.
- Vitamin B12: fasting for 6 to 8 hours is preferred.
- During fasting, the patient must not take tea, coffee, juice, chewing gum or smoke.
- Patients should take their regular medicines unless their doctor has said otherwise,
  but diabetic patients must not take diabetes medicine until after the fasting sample.

3.2 Post prandial blood sugar (PPBS)
- Collect exactly 2 hours after the patient starts their meal.
- Write the meal time and collection time on the request form.

3.3 Tests that do NOT need fasting
- HbA1c, Complete Blood Count, Thyroid Profile, TSH, Vitamin D, CRP and most serology
  tests can be collected at any time of day.

3.4 Urine samples
- Give the patient a sterile screw-cap container.
- Explain clean-catch midstream collection: pass the first part of urine into the
  toilet, collect the middle part in the container, and close the lid tightly.
- First morning urine is preferred for urine routine.
- For urine culture, collect before starting any antibiotics.
- The sample must reach the laboratory within 2 hours.

3.5 Special cases
- Patients on warfarin: write the current dose on the PT/INR request form.
- Thyroid patients: collect the sample before the morning thyroid tablet where possible.
- HIV testing: written informed consent must be signed before collection. Results are
  given only to the patient or the referring doctor in person, never by phone.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHAT PATIENTS SHOULD BRING
------------------------------------------------------------
- Doctor's prescription or request form.
- A government photo ID.
- Previous reports, if the doctor wants a comparison.
- List of current medicines.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. REPORT DELIVERY
------------------------------------------------------------
- Turnaround times are listed in the BIODNA test catalog (test master).
- Reports are sent by email and SMS link as soon as they are approved by the pathologist.
- Printed reports can be collected from the front desk with the bill receipt.
- Reports are never shared with anyone other than the patient or the referring doctor
  without the patient's written permission.
- Critical results are phoned directly to the treating doctor by the laboratory,
  as per BIODNA-QM-REF-001. Front office staff must not give critical results to
  patients over the phone.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. RECOLLECTION
------------------------------------------------------------
- If a sample is rejected (for example haemolysed, clotted or wrongly labelled),
  the front office calls the patient within 1 hour to arrange recollection.
- Recollection is free of charge.
- Explain the reason politely without blaming the patient or the phlebotomist.

------------------------------------------------------------
7. WHAT FRONT OFFICE STAFF MUST NOT DO
------------------------------------------------------------
- Do not interpret results or tell a patient whether a result is "normal" or "bad".
- Do not suggest medicines or treatment.
- Do not change a doctor's test request without speaking to the doctor.
- For any medical question, politely ask the patient to consult their doctor or
  request a call back from the consultant pathologist.

------------------------------------------------------------
8. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Can I drink water before a fasting test?
A: Yes. Plain water is allowed and helps make blood collection easier.

Q: Do I need to fast for HbA1c?
A: No. HbA1c reflects average blood sugar over about 3 months and can be done at any time.

Q: How long does a thyroid profile report take?
A: 24 hours from sample receipt. See the test catalog for other tests.

Q: Can a family member collect my report?
A: Only with a signed authorisation letter from the patient and the bill receipt.

Q: My sample was rejected. Do I need to pay again?
A: No. Recollection after a rejected sample is free of charge.
